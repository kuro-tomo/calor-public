Calor

Open-source calibration of Arrhenius thermal-runaway kinetics (Ea, A, ΔH, Cp) for lithium-ion BESS cells, fit directly to public Accelerating Rate Calorimetry (ARC) data.

Who this is for

Built for PE and FPE firms performing large-scale fire test (LSFT) analysis and thermal-runaway hazard modeling under NFPA 855 — as a white-label calibration component feeding downstream tools such as FLACS or KFX, not a stamped deliverable in its own right.
